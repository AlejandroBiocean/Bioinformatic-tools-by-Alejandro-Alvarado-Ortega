## About Projects

This repository have and develop bioinformatic tools, scripts and pipelines. Include different omics analysis. 

Now are avaible:
_ DADA2 for ASVs analysis.
_ DESeq2 for differential expression analysis. 

## Requirements

_ Conda or Mamba.
_ R and ggplot2, DESeq2, tximport, DADA2, phyloseq, dplyr & Biostrings packages.
_ Linux (WSL could be an option for Windows PC).
